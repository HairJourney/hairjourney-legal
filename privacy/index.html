HairJourney Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 4, 2026 Last updated: 4 November 2026

HairJourney ("HairJourney", "we", "us") is a hair-care app for Android. This policy explains what information the app collects, why, who processes it for us, and the choices you have. HairJourney is operated by Rezi'sDeck (Anita Obieroma).

1. Information we collect

Information you give us

  • Account details: your name and email address, received from Google when you sign in with Google.
  • Profile: display name, profile photo, hair type and texture, hair goals, gender presentation (used to tailor style suggestions and Hair Twin matching), and any bio you add.
  • Photos: hair journey and progress photos, post images, and photos you choose to use in Style Studio.
  • Journey and calendar entries: care routines, logs, notes and completed tasks.
  • Community content: posts, comments, hashtags, YouTube links you share, likes, follows, and challenges you create or join.
  • Messages: chats with stylists and other users.
  • Stylist Virtual Shops and bookings: shop details, services, prices and availability (for stylists), and booking requests, dates and status (for clients and stylists).
  • Products: products you add to My Products, including barcode numbers and text read from product labels.
  • Bella AI assistant: questions you send to Bella and her replies.

Information collected automatically

  • Push notification token: a Firebase Cloud Messaging (FCM) device token so we can send notifications you have enabled.
  • Purchase records: when you buy a subscription (Plus, Pro, Ultra) or a credit pack, Google Play processes the payment. We receive a purchase token, product, status and dates to verify and unlock your purchase, and we keep a record of credit use. We never receive or store your card or bank details.
  • Technical data: basic logs (such as time of request, error messages and app version) kept by our hosting providers to run and secure the service.

We do not collect your precise location, contacts, or advertising ID, and the app does not show third-party ads.

2. How we use information

  • To create and run your account and sign you in.
  • To show your journey, calendar, products and Style Studio results.
  • To run the Community, challenges, Hair Twin matching, messaging, stylist shops and bookings.
  • To provide AI features (Bella, AI style try-on, label reading) when you use them.
  • To verify purchases and manage subscriptions and credits.
  • To send notifications you have turned on.
  • To keep the service safe, prevent abuse, fix bugs and respond to reports.

We do not use your photos or messages to train our own AI models.

4. Sharing

  • We do not sell your personal information, and we do not share it for advertising.
  • Other users see what you choose to make visible: your public profile, posts, comments, challenge participation, and (for stylists) shop details. Messages are visible to the people in the conversation. Booking details are shared between the client and the stylist.
  • Legal reasons: we may disclose information if required by law or to protect users' safety.

5. Retention

We keep your information while your account is active. When you delete your account, we delete or anonymise your personal data within 30 days, except purchase records we must keep for tax, accounting or fraud-prevention reasons, and backups that expire on their normal cycle.

6. Your choices and rights

  • Edit or delete posts, photos, products and journey entries in the app.
  • Turn notifications off in the app or in Android settings.
  • Delete your account and data: request deletion in the app via Profile > Settings if available, or email anitaoreziobi@gmail.com with the subject "Delete my HairJourney account" from the email linked to your account. Your data is deleted within 30 days.
  • Ask for a copy of your data or a correction by emailing us. Depending on where you live (for example under the GDPR), you may have further rights, including to object or complain to your local data-protection authority.
  • Subscriptions are managed and cancelled in Google Play → Payments & subscriptions. Deleting the app does not cancel a subscription.

7. Children

HairJourney is not directed at children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them. Children's hairstyles in Style Studio are meant for parents and caregivers to use. If you believe a child under 13 has created an account, contact us and we will delete it.

8. Security

Data is sent over encrypted connections (HTTPS), and access to stored data is restricted by account-level access rules. No system is perfectly secure, so please use a secure Google account.

9. Changes

We will update this page when our practices change and change the "Last updated" date. For significant changes we will notify you in the app.
